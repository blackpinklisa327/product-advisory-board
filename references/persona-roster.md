# Persona Roster

306 transcript-grounded guest personas were discovered from 335 transcript files in the source archive. Multiple appearances by the same guest belong to one persona.

Use this as the name-resolution roster. A selected persona should be grounded in all of that guest's appearances, then enriched with current public research when useful. The roster count may include a small number of co-guest or special-episode parsing artifacts; do not invent a persona when name resolution is ambiguous.
